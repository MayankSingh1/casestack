# Senior Case Study Rubric

A comprehensive scoring system for evaluating design portfolio case studies at the senior level.

## Seniority Signals

What hiring managers look for when evaluating senior designers:

### 1. Ownership (0-3 points)

**0 - No ownership shown**
- Tasks described without accountability
- "I was assigned to..." framing

**1 - Execution ownership**
- Owned specific deliverables
- Limited scope of influence

**2 - Project ownership**
- Owned end-to-end design decisions
- Set direction within defined scope

**3 - Strategic ownership**
- Defined the approach and scope
- Influenced beyond immediate project
- Made decisions that shaped product direction

**Evidence to look for:**
- "I defined the design strategy"
- "I decided to prioritize X over Y"
- "I proposed we take this approach"

### 2. Strategy (0-3 points)

**0 - No strategic thinking**
- Only execution details
- No connection to business goals

**1 - Awareness of strategy**
- Mentions business context
- Understands goals but didn't shape them

**2 - Strategic participation**
- Contributed to strategic decisions
- Framed problems with business impact in mind

**3 - Strategic leadership**
- Shaped product strategy
- Connected design decisions to business outcomes
- Influenced roadmap or priorities

**Evidence to look for:**
- Business metrics tied to design decisions
- Problem reframing that changed direction
- Trade-offs evaluated against business impact

### 3. Influence (0-3 points)

**0 - No influence beyond self**
- Solo contributor work only
- No collaboration mentioned

**1 - Team influence**
- Worked with immediate team
- Influenced direct collaborators

**2 - Cross-functional influence**
- Changed minds across teams
- Stakeholder alignment achieved
- Mentored or led others

**3 - Organizational influence**
- Shaped practices beyond project
- Created frameworks others adopted
- Changed how teams work

**Evidence to look for:**
- "I convinced stakeholders that..."
- "This approach was adopted by other teams"
- "I mentored junior designers on..."

### 4. Measurable Impact (0-3 points)

**0 - No impact mentioned**
- No outcomes described
- Only process shown

**1 - Qualitative impact**
- Positive reception mentioned
- No numbers provided

**2 - Quantified impact**
- Metrics provided with numbers
- Before/after comparison

**3 - Significant quantified impact**
- Multiple metrics with meaningful change
- Clear business value articulated
- Impact exceeds typical expectations

**Evidence to look for:**
- Specific percentages and numbers
- Revenue or cost impact
- User behavior changes measured

### 5. Craft (0-3 points)

**0 - Poor craft**
- Sloppy visuals
- Inconsistent execution
- Missing details

**1 - Adequate craft**
- Clean execution
- Follows conventions
- No major issues

**2 - Strong craft**
- Polished visuals
- Attention to details
- Consistent system

**3 - Exceptional craft**
- Standout visual quality
- Innovative solutions
- Sweats the details others miss

**Evidence to look for:**
- Micro-interactions considered
- Edge cases handled
- Visual polish and consistency

### 6. Collaboration (0-3 points)

**0 - Solo work only**
- No collaboration mentioned
- Isolated contribution

**1 - Basic collaboration**
- Worked with team
- Handed off work

**2 - Active collaboration**
- Cross-functional partnership
- Facilitated alignment
- Gave and received feedback

**3 - Collaborative leadership**
- Led cross-functional initiatives
- Built relationships that unblocked work
- Created processes for better collaboration

**Evidence to look for:**
- Working with specific roles (eng, PM, research)
- Facilitating workshops or reviews
- Resolving conflicts or misalignment

### 7. Growth Mindset (0-3 points)

**0 - No reflection**
- No learnings mentioned
- No self-awareness

**1 - Basic reflection**
- Mentions what was learned
- Generic insights

**2 - Meaningful reflection**
- Specific learnings articulated
- What they'd change identified
- Growth evident

**3 - Deep reflection**
- Transformed their practice
- Honest about failures and pivots
- Advice for others

**Evidence to look for:**
- "I learned that..."
- "If I did this again, I'd..."
- Honest admission of challenges

## Section Scoring

### Hero Section (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | Missing or incomplete |
| 1 | Basic info present, weak hook |
| 2 | Complete info, decent hook |
| 3 | Compelling hook, clear outcome, all meta |

**Must include:**
- Title
- One-line outcome
- Role
- Timeline
- Team
- Platform

### TL;DR (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | Missing |
| 1 | Present but too long or vague |
| 2 | Concise, hits main points |
| 3 | Punchy, outcome-first, scannable |

**Must be:**
- Under 80 words
- Problem → approach → outcome structure
- Lead with impact

### Context & Business Problem (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | Missing or unclear |
| 1 | Problem stated, no business context |
| 2 | Clear problem with business stakes |
| 3 | Compelling business case, stakes clear |

**Must include:**
- What the business/product does
- The specific problem
- Why it mattered (stakes)

### My Role & Collaboration (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | Missing or vague |
| 1 | Role stated, no detail |
| 2 | Role clear, some ownership detail |
| 3 | Specific ownership, collaboration clear |

**Must include:**
- Title/role
- What you personally owned
- Who you worked with

### Constraints (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No constraints mentioned |
| 1 | Generic constraints (tight timeline) |
| 2 | Specific constraints listed |
| 3 | Constraints shaped decisions, well articulated |

**Must include:**
- At least 2-3 real constraints
- Specific, not generic

### Research & Insights (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No research mentioned |
| 1 | Methods listed, thin findings |
| 2 | Methods + findings + some evidence |
| 3 | Rigorous research, findings with quotes/data |

**Must include:**
- Methods used
- Key findings (3-5)
- Evidence (quotes, numbers)

### Problem Framing (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | Missing |
| 1 | Brief problem statement |
| 2 | Reframed problem, success criteria |
| 3 | HMWs, clear metrics, upfront targets |

**Should include:**
- Reframed problem statement
- How Might We questions
- Success metrics with targets

### Process & Key Decisions (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | Missing or chronological dump |
| 1 | Decisions mentioned, no alternatives |
| 2 | Decisions with some rationale |
| 3 | Alternatives, trade-offs, clear rationale |

**Must include:**
- Key decisions (2-4)
- Alternatives considered
- Trade-offs acknowledged
- Rationale for choice

### Solution Walkthrough (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No visuals or description |
| 1 | Basic description, few visuals |
| 2 | Clear walkthrough, annotated visuals |
| 3 | Rich visuals, tied to insights, polished |

**Must include:**
- Visual for each major feature
- Annotations explaining why
- Connection to research findings

### Validation (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No testing mentioned |
| 1 | Testing mentioned, no details |
| 2 | Testing methods + what changed |
| 3 | Rigorous testing, clear iteration story |

**Must include:**
- Testing methods used
- What was learned
- What changed as result

### Impact & Outcomes (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No outcomes |
| 1 | Qualitative outcomes only |
| 2 | Quantified metrics |
| 3 | Multiple metrics, business value clear |

**Must include:**
- At least one quantified metric
- Before/after comparison
- Business context for numbers

### Reflection & Learnings (0-3 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | Missing |
| 1 | Generic learnings |
| 2 | Specific learnings |
| 3 | Deep insights, what you'd change, honest |

**Must include:**
- What was learned
- What you'd do differently

## Presentation Patterns (Bonus Points)

Based on analysis of 12 senior designer portfolios, these patterns elevate case studies:

### Outcome-First Framing (+1)
- Impact visible in first viewport
- Lead with result, not process
- Hero includes key metric

### Clear Progression (+1)
- Phases/steps numbered (1/3, 2/3, 3/3)
- Creates narrative arc
- Reader knows where they are in the story

### Visual Evidence (+1)
- Solution sections show, not just tell
- Annotated screenshots
- Before/after comparisons

### Constraints Honesty (+1)
- Limitations acknowledged explicitly
- What couldn't be done is stated
- Time/resource constraints mentioned

### Forward Thinking (+1)
- "What's next" section included
- Future improvements documented
- Shows strategic thinking beyond launch

### Interaction Quality (0-3)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | Static page, no interactivity |
| 1 | Basic scroll reveals |
| 2 | Meaningful interactions (galleries, comparisons) |
| 3 | Intentional narrative interactions (scrollytelling, pinned sections) |

**Best-in-class examples:**
- Sanvithi-style pinned scrollytelling
- Image comparison sliders
- Animated metrics on scroll
- Horizontal galleries for visual work

### Mobile Responsiveness (+1)
- All sections work on mobile
- Complex interactions collapse appropriately
- Navigation adapts to small screens

### Accessibility (+1)
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected
- Keyboard navigation works
- Focus states visible
- WCAG AA contrast ratios

## Red Flags (Immediate Concerns)

- **No metrics**: Even relative ones matter
- **"I did everything"**: Inflated ownership
- **No trade-offs**: Perfect stories aren't credible
- **Chronological process dump**: Missing strategic framing
- **Wall of text**: No visual breaks
- **Generic learnings**: "I learned to communicate better"
- **No user evidence**: No research or quotes
- **Static, unengaging presentation**: No scroll interactions or visual polish
- **Ignores reduced motion**: Animations don't respect accessibility preferences
- **Broken on mobile**: Desktop-only layout
